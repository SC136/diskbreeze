Read the safety notes

Protected paths

Every delete passes is_protected: drive roots, Windows, your profile folders, Program Files.

Fixed admin steps

Admin commands live in the catalog, are passed on the command line, and are pinned by a test.

No file crawling out

Symlinks and junctions are never followed. OneDrive cloud-only files count as zero bytes.

Local history

Every cleanup is logged on your machine: what, when, how much, and what failed.
